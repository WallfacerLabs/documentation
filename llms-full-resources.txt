# Why use vaults.fyi
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/why-vaultsfyi

> Decide whether vaults.fyi fits your product: what it replaces, who uses it, which capabilities are GA (v2) vs Beta, and the cheapest first integration for each use case.

This page helps you decide whether vaults.fyi fits your product and which part of the API to start with. It covers what vaults.fyi replaces, who it is for, which capabilities are generally available (v2) versus Beta, and a concrete first integration per use case. vaults.fyi is a REST API at `https://api.vaults.fyi` (key in the `x-api-key` header) that returns normalized data for 3,000+ DeFi yield vaults on 80+ protocols and 22 EVM networks, builds unsigned vault transactions, and reports wallet positions. Solana vaults are available on the Beta SVM endpoints.

## What vaults.fyi replaces

| Without vaults.fyi | With vaults.fyi |
| - | - |
| One adapter, transaction builder and data pipeline per protocol | One schema for every vault (`GET /v2/detailed-vaults`) and one transaction pattern (`context` → `{action}`) |
| Protocol-reported or forward-looking APYs that are not comparable | Realized APY computed the same way for every vault from onchain share prices, net of fees, in five windows (`1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day`) |
| A hand-maintained allowlist for risk screening | Reputation Score (`score.vaultScore`), `flags`, `warnings`, holder data and benchmarks to screen with |
| No view after the user deposits | Positions, returns, events, idle tokens and reward claims per wallet (`/v2/portfolio/*`) |

## Who uses it

### Wallets, exchanges and consumer apps

- Turn idle wallet balances into deposit suggestions (`GET /v2/portfolio/idle-assets/{userAddress}`, `GET /v2/portfolio/best-deposit-options/{userAddress}`).
- Let users deposit, redeem and claim rewards without custody: the API returns unsigned calls that the user's wallet signs and sends directly to the protocol contract.
- Show positions, returns and history after deposit.
- Measure the TVL your app drives with the attribution suffix ([TVL attribution](/concepts/tvl-attribution)).

Guide: [For wallets and apps](/use-cases/for-wallets-and-apps).

### Analysts and data teams

- Query APY, TVL, fees, rewards and metadata across the market without running an indexer.
- Separate base yield from reward yield: `apy.<window>.base` and `apy.<window>.reward` for the windows `1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day` and `90day` (the per-vault `/apy` endpoint returns `1day` to `90day` only).
- Pull historical APY, TVL and share price (`GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}`).
- Compare against USD and ETH benchmark rates (`GET /v2/benchmarks/{network}?code=usd`).

Guide: [For analysts](/use-cases/for-analysts).

### Protocols and curators

- Get your vaults in front of the wallets and apps that integrate vaults.fyi (listing: see [FAQ](/resources/faq)).
- See which partners route deposits into your vaults via the attribution portal.
- Show performance against benchmarks.

Guide: [For protocols](/use-cases/for-protocols).

### AI agents

- Discover and compare vaults with structured filters, then fetch transaction context and unsigned payloads for the user to approve.
- Use the hosted MCP server (`Authorization: Bearer <key>`) or REST (`x-api-key`), or pay per request with x402 and no key.

Guides: [AI agents overview](/ai-agents/overview), [Rules for agents](/ai-agents/rules-for-agents), [Transaction safety](/ai-agents/transaction-safety).

## Start small: the first integration per use case

| Goal | First calls | Credit cost (from [Credits](/plans/credits)) |

# Embed Yield and Borrowing in Wallets and Consumer Apps
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/use-cases/for-wallets-and-apps

> Give users earn and borrow features with the data, risk context, transaction payloads, portfolio tracking, attribution, and fee logic your team needs.

Vaults.fyi gives wallets, exchanges, neobanks, fintech apps, portfolio tools, and aggregators the building blocks for embedded earn and borrow experiences across 80+ protocols, 20+ EVM chains, and Solana/SVM vault coverage in Beta. Use normalized vault and market data as the foundation, then add transaction payloads, portfolio tracking, borrowing views, attribution, or fee logic as your product evolves.

## Why it matters

Consumer platforms already have the user relationship, asset context, and trust surface for earn and borrow features. The hard part is turning fragmented DeFi markets into product-safe options: which opportunities to show, how to explain them, how to route the user, and how to track what happens after capital moves.

Vaults.fyi gives your team the components to do that without rebuilding the market stack. Start with ranked vaults, APY windows, TVL, protocol metadata, rewards, fees, capacity, and risk/return context. Then add the workflows that matter for your roadmap: non-custodial execution, portfolio tracking, TVL attribution, borrowing views, and monetization.

Use Vaults.fyi to:

* Match yield opportunities to the assets a user already holds.
* Filter out vaults that do not meet your risk/return policy.
* Compare APY, TVL, fees, rewards, capacity, curators, networks, and protocols with one schema.
* Prepare non-custodial transactions for deposits, withdrawals, and reward claims when you want execution.
* Show position balances, returns, and history when you want portfolio tracking.
* Measure retained TVL when you want partner reporting, rebates, or commercial attribution.

## What you can build

- [Yield discovery](/concepts/vault-discovery.md): Surface ranked vault opportunities filtered by asset, network, APY, and reputation score. Users see exactly where their idle funds can earn.

- [Embeddable widget](/use-cases/embeddable-widget.md): Launch a branded Earn surface with curated discovery, positions, and transaction-ready flows without designing the full UI from scratch.

- [Data-only yield views](/api-reference/detailed-vaults.md): Build discovery screens, internal allowlists, comparison tables, or routing logic from normalized vault data without adding transactions.

- [1-click deposits and withdrawals](/concepts/transaction-flows.md): Generate ready-to-sign deposit and redeem payloads for any supported vault. Transactions go directly from the user's wallet to the target protocol.

- [Portfolio tracking](/concepts/portfolio-tracking.md): Show users their current positions, accrued returns, and PnL across all yield strategies they've entered through your app.

- [Borrowing](/concepts/borrowing.md): Show borrow markets, supplied and borrowed positions, risk metrics, and supported supply, withdraw, borrow, and repay actions.

- [TVL attribution](/concepts/tvl-attribution.md): Track the deposits, withdrawals, and retained TVL your app drives into each vault, protocol, curator, and network.

- [Monetization](#monetize-yield-integrations): Attach flat or percentage-based fees to deposit and withdrawal transactions. Configure fee logic through the API without touching smart contracts.

## How integration works

Most teams start by fetching vault data. The later steps are optional modules you can add when your product needs execution, attribution, portfolio tracking, or borrowing.

**1. Fetch vaults**

Call `GET /v2/detailed-vaults` to retrieve a filtered, ranked list of vaults with APY, TVL, the Reputation Score, and protocol metadata. Apply filters for asset, network, or minimum reputation score to match your users' needs.

**2. Optional: generate a transaction payload**

Call the transactions endpoint with the vault address, user wallet, and deposit amount. The API returns a ready-to-sign payload with no intermediary contracts involved.

**3. Optional: sign and submit**

Pass the payload to the user's wallet for signing. Your app broadcasts the transaction. The user interacts directly with the target protocol.

**4. Optional: track attributed TVL**

Transactions generated through Vaults.fyi include TVL tracking automatically, so you can measure retained balances and vault-level flows in portal.vaults.fyi. If you build vault transactions yourself, append a free attribution suffix instead. Learn more in [TVL Attribution](/concepts/tvl-attribution.md).

**5. Optional: track positions**

Use the portfolio endpoints to display the user's current balance in each vault, yield earned since deposit, and estimated annual return.

**6. Optional: add borrowing views**

Use the Beta borrowing API to show supported borrow markets, supplied and borrowed positions, risk metrics, and available supply, withdraw, borrow, or repay actions.

## Non-custodial by design

All transaction payloads route funds directly from the user's wallet to the target protocol. Vaults.fyi never holds or controls user funds. This means:

* No intermediary smart contracts inflating gas costs
* Fewer attack surfaces compared to proxy or aggregator architectures
* Full UX control stays with your app

> [!NOTE]
>
> Both externally owned accounts (EOAs) and smart wallets are supported. For smart wallets, you can bundle fee transactions with the user's deposit in a single atomic operation.

## Monetize yield integrations

You can attach fees to any deposit or withdrawal routed through your app. The API supports multiple fee structures so you can design the model that fits your business.

- [Flat fees](#monetize-yield-integrations): Charge a fixed amount per deposit or withdrawal, regardless of transaction size.

- [Performance fees](#monetize-yield-integrations): Take a percentage of yield earned over time, aligning your revenue with user outcomes.

Fee logic is implemented directly in the API payload. No custom smart contract development required. For bespoke fee modules, the Vaults.fyi team works with you to design and validate the implementation.

> [!TIP]
>
> With EIP-7702, you can implement fee flows for traditional EOAs without requiring users to migrate to a smart wallet. This expands your monetization reach across all wallet types.

## Who's already using this

[Arbitrum Earn](/resources/case-studies/arbitrum.md) uses Vaults.fyi to bring native yield discovery and execution into the Arbitrum Portal. [Kraken Wallet](https://www.kraken.com/wallet) surfaces yield opportunities directly inside the wallet interface, while [Jumper Earn](https://jumper.exchange/) integrates the same API to power its cross-chain yield aggregation experience.

# vaults.fyi for analysts and researchers: normalized yield, TVL and benchmark data
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/use-cases/for-analysts

> How analysts use vaults.fyi: normalized APY (base, reward, total; five windows), TVL, Reputation Score and benchmark rates across 3,000+ vaults, with full-market pull guidance (all networks, minTvl=0, nextPage), daily history and credit budgets; tested Python and TypeScript examples.

This page explains what data analysts, researchers and allocators can pull from vaults.fyi, which endpoints to use, how to avoid silently incomplete datasets, and what a pull costs. Use it to plan a dashboard, backtest or report; the linked recipes and tutorial contain tested code.

All REST requests go to `https://api.vaults.fyi` with the header `x-api-key: $VAULTSFYI_API_KEY` (not `Authorization: Bearer`, which is only for the MCP server). APY values are decimal fractions (`0.0315` = 3.15 %), net of fees. Timestamps are Unix seconds. The index refreshes about hourly.

## What data is available

| Data | Fields | Notes |
| - | - | - |
| APY | `apy.<window>.{base, reward, total}`, windows `1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day` | Realized share-price yield plus rewards; `apyComposite` on nested vaults ([calculating APY](/methodology/calculating-apy)) |
| TVL | `tvl.usd` (integer-USD string), `tvl.native` (base units) | |
| Reputation Score | `score.vaultScore` (0 to 100, unrounded; observed 27.5–99.1) and four components: protocol TVL history 45 %, vault TVL history 25 %, holder distribution 15 %, asset 15 % | [Reputation Score](/methodology/reputation-score) |
| Benchmarks | USD and ETH benchmark per network: the TVL-weighted APY of the five largest vaults of that denomination | [Benchmark rates](/methodology/benchmark-rates) |
| History | APY, TVL, share price per vault; asset prices; benchmarks | Oldest first; `granularity` `1hour` (default), `1day`, `1week` |
| Holders | `holdersData` on detailed vaults; per-holder list on Alpha | [Alpha](/api-reference/alpha) |
| Utilization, drawdown, composition | Beta advanced analytics | [Beta](/api-reference/beta) |

## Key endpoints

| Endpoint | Returns | Credits |
| - | - | - |
| `GET /v2/vaults` | Identity of every vault (all networks by default), no metrics | 1 per call |
| `GET /v2/detailed-vaults` | Current APY, TVL, score, rewards, fees, warnings | 1 + 3 per vault |
| `GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}/apy` (also `/tvl`, `/sharePrice`) | One series per vault | 1 + 1 per point |
| `GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}` | APY, TVL and share price together | 3 + 3 per point |
| `GET /v2/benchmarks/{network}?code=usd` | Current benchmark (`code` required: `usd` or `eth`) | 3 |
| `GET /v2/historical-benchmarks/{network}?code=usd` | Benchmark history | 3 + 3 per point |
| `GET /v2/portfolio/positions/{userAddress}` | One wallet's current vault positions | 5 |

## Full-market pulls: avoid silent gaps

`/v2/detailed-vaults` searches only base, mainnet, arbitrum and optimism and only vaults with TVL ≥ $100,000 unless told otherwise. For a full dataset:

- pass every network as a repeated `allowedNetworks` key (take names from `/v2/vaults`, which lists all networks with vaults),
- set `minTvl=0`,
- page with `page=0,1,…` until `nextPage` is absent (there is no total count),
- treat non-empty `errors.unsupported*` as a failed query.

Budget: about 3 credits per vault, so all 3,662 vaults ≈ 11,000 credits per snapshot; cheaper: list identities with `/v2/vaults` (1 credit) and fetch detail for the subset you study. Respect `x-ratelimit-remaining` and `x-ratelimit-reset` ([errors](/api-reference/errors#429-rate-limit-exceeded)).

## Fetch 90 days of daily APY

Tested Python (REST), from the [vault history vs benchmark](/recipes/vault-history-vs-benchmark) recipe:

```python
"""90 days of daily APY for one vault, compared with the network's USD benchmark."""
import os
import time

import requests

API = "https://api.vaults.fyi"
HEADERS = {"x-api-key": os.environ["VAULTSFYI_API_KEY"]}
NETWORK, VAULT_ID = "base", "0xbeeff2490FEffa212faC2f6553682C219E6a8845"  # Steakhouse High Yield USDC

to_ts = int(time.time()) // 86400 * 86400  # Unix SECONDS (milliseconds -> 400)
from_ts = to_ts - 90 * 86400

# 1) History. granularity defaults to 1hour and perPage to 50: set both or you get 50 hourly points.
points, page = [], 0
while True:
    r = requests.get(
        f"{API}/v2/historical/{NETWORK}/{VAULT_ID}/apy",
        headers=HEADERS,
        params={"granularity": "1day", "apyInterval": "7day", "fromTimestamp": from_ts,
                "toTimestamp": to_ts, "perPage": 100, "page": page},
        timeout=30,
    )
    r.raise_for_status()
    body = r.json()
    points += body["data"]  # oldest first
    if "nextPage" not in body:
        break
    page = body["nextPage"]
print(f"{len(points)} daily points, cost ~{1 + len(points)} credits (1 per call + 1 per point)")
print("first", time.strftime("%Y-%m-%d", time.gmtime(points[0]["timestamp"])), f'{points[0]["apy"]["total"] * 100:.2f} %')
print("last ", time.strftime("%Y-%m-%d", time.gmtime(points[-1]["timestamp"])), f'{points[-1]["apy"]["total"] * 100:.2f} %')

# 2) Current vault APY and the benchmark, SAME window (7day). code is required: usd or eth.
vault = requests.get(f"{API}/v2/detailed-vaults/{NETWORK}/{VAULT_ID}", headers=HEADERS, timeout=30).json()
bench = requests.get(f"{API}/v2/benchmarks/{NETWORK}", headers=HEADERS, params={"code": "usd"}, timeout=30).json()
v7, b7 = vault["apy"]["7day"]["total"], bench["apy"]["7day"]["total"]
spread_bp = round((v7 - b7) * 10_000)  # decimal fractions -> basis points
print(f"vault 7d {v7 * 100:.2f} % vs USD benchmark 7d {b7 * 100:.2f} % -> {spread_bp:+d} bp "
      f'({"beating" if v7 > b7 else "below"} benchmark)')
```

Output (2026-10-23):

```text
90 daily points, cost ~91 credits (1 per call + 1 per point)
first 2026-07-25 2.54 %
last  2026-10-23 3.15 %
vault 7d 3.15 % vs USD benchmark 7d 4.07 % -> -92 bp (below benchmark)
```

TypeScript, the same recipe:

```typescript
// 90 days of daily APY for one vault, compared with the network's USD benchmark.
const API = 'https://api.vaults.fyi';
const headers = { 'x-api-key': process.env.VAULTSFYI_API_KEY! };
const NETWORK = 'base';
const VAULT_ID = '0xbeeff2490FEffa212faC2f6553682C219E6a8845'; // Steakhouse High Yield USDC

async function get(path: string, params: Record<string, string | number> = {}) {
  const qs = new URLSearchParams(Object.entries(params).map(([k, v]) => [k, String(v)]));
  const res = await fetch(`${API}${path}${qs.size ? `?${qs}` : ''}`, { headers });
  if (!res.ok) throw new Error(`HTTP ${res.status}: ${await res.text()}`);
  return res.json();
}

const toTs = Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000 / 86400) * 86400; // Unix SECONDS, midnight UTC
const fromTs = toTs - 90 * 86400;

type Point = { timestamp: number; apy: { base: number; reward: number; total: number } };
const points: Point[] = [];
let page = 0;
while (true) {
  // granularity defaults to 1hour and perPage to 50 -- set both explicitly.
  const body = await get(`/v2/historical/${NETWORK}/${VAULT_ID}/apy`, {
    granularity: '1day', apyInterval: '7day', fromTimestamp: fromTs, toTimestamp: toTs, perPage: 100, page,
  });
  points.push(...body.data); // oldest first
  if (body.nextPage === undefined) break;
  page = body.nextPage;
}
const day = (t: number) => new Date(t * 1000).toISOString().slice(0, 10);
console.log(`${points.length} daily points, cost ~${1 + points.length} credits`);
console.log('first', day(points[0].timestamp), `${(points[0].apy.total * 100).toFixed(2)} %`);
console.log('last ', day(points.at(-1)!.timestamp), `${(points.at(-1)!.apy.total * 100).toFixed(2)} %`);

const vault = await get(`/v2/detailed-vaults/${NETWORK}/${VAULT_ID}`);
const bench = await get(`/v2/benchmarks/${NETWORK}`, { code: 'usd' }); // code is required
const v7: number = vault.apy['7day'].total;
const b7: number = bench.apy['7day'].total;
const spreadBp = Math.round((v7 - b7) * 10_000);
console.log(`vault 7d ${(v7 * 100).toFixed(2)} % vs USD benchmark 7d ${(b7 * 100).toFixed(2)} % -> ${spreadBp >= 0 ? '+' : ''}${spreadBp} bp (${v7 > b7 ? 'beating' : 'below'} benchmark)`);
```

What changed from the previous version of this page: the key goes in `x-api-key` (the old `Authorization: Bearer` header gets a 402), `granularity=1day` is set (the default hourly granularity returned only the oldest few days), the field is `apy.reward` (not `apy.rewards`), and the example uses a real vault instead of `0xBEEF...`.

## Research notes

- **APY windows are realized yields.** `7day` is the annualized share-price growth over the last 7 days plus rewards; not a forecast.
- **APY is unclamped.** Small vaults produce extreme values; filter by `minTvl` and score.
- **Strict score thresholds:** check `score.vaultScore` client-side if your study needs one.
- **Benchmarks are not risk-adjusted.** They are TVL-weighted averages of the top five vaults.
- **History starts at each vault's indexing start**; page 0 is the oldest data.

# vaults.fyi for protocols and curators: get listed, check your vault data, track attribution
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/use-cases/for-protocols

> How protocols and curators get vaults listed on vaults.fyi (protocol request form, onchain data requirements), check how a vault appears (GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}, score, warnings), read holders and flows, and see partner-attributed TVL in portal.vaults.fyi.

This page explains how a protocol or curator gets vaults into vaults.fyi, how to check what integrators see about a vault, and how to measure the capital partners route to it. Use it when you want wallets, apps, analysts and agents building on vaults.fyi to surface your vaults.

REST requests go to `https://api.vaults.fyi` with the header `x-api-key: $VAULTSFYI_API_KEY`. APY values are decimal fractions net of fees.

## Why it matters

Every integration built on the API (wallets, Earn surfaces, aggregators, analytics tools, agents) queries the same normalized dataset. A listed vault appears in their results when it matches their filters: asset, network, TVL, Reputation Score, warnings and APY. Teams using vaults.fyi include [Arbitrum Earn](/resources/case-studies/arbitrum), [Jumper Earn](/resources/case-studies/jumper-earn), [Kraken Wallet](/resources/case-studies/kraken-wallet), [Gauntlet](/resources/case-studies/gauntlet), [Maple Finance](/resources/case-studies/maple-finance) and [KPK](/resources/case-studies/kpk).

## Getting listed

1. **Check the data requirements.** vaults.fyi indexes vaults from onchain data: TVL, share supply, price per share, deposit limit, available liquidity, the underlying token, the share token and reward emissions must be readable from the vault's contracts (ERC-4626 vaults expose most of this). The full list is in the [FAQ](/resources/faq#what-does-a-vault-need-to-be-listed). Priority goes to protocols with significant traction and customer demand.
2. **Submit the protocol.** Use the [protocol request form](https://listings.vaults.fyi/), or contact [@vaultsfyi](https://twitter.com/vaultsfyi) on X or Farcaster. The team reviews each protocol before integrating it.
3. **Verify indexing.** Once listed, check the vault with the call below.

Once a protocol and network are supported, its vaults are listed automatically when the deposit asset is on the reviewed asset list, the vault holds non-trivial TVL, and it has produced fresh hourly data in the last 60 days. Audit status is not a listing input. Earlier versions of this page listed an audit requirement and a minimum TVL; those were wrong.

## Check how your vault appears

Look the vault up directly by network and `vaultId` (3 credits). There is no "filter by vault address" on the list endpoint: unknown query parameters are ignored and you would get the default list.

```bash
# Check how your vault appears in the API: one vault by network + vaultId (3 credits).
curl -sS --fail-with-body "https://api.vaults.fyi/v2/detailed-vaults/base/0xbeeff2490FEffa212faC2f6553682C219E6a8845" \
  -H "x-api-key: $VAULTSFYI_API_KEY" \
| jq '{name, protocol: .protocol.name, isTransactional, apy7d: .apy["7day"].total, tvlUsd: .tvl.usd,
       vaultScore: .score.vaultScore, warnings: [.warnings[]?], holders: .holdersData.totalCount}'
```

Output (2026-10-23, Steakhouse High Yield USDC on Base):

```json
{
  "name": "Steakhouse High Yield USDC Edition",
  "protocol": "morpho",
  "isTransactional": true,
  "apy7d": 0.0315,
  "tvlUsd": "468440351",
  "vaultScore": 81.7485752746402,
  "warnings": [],
  "holders": 45152
}
```

What integrators filter on, and where it comes from:

| Field | Meaning |
| - | - |
| `apy.<window>.total` | Realized yield (share price + rewards), net of fees ([calculating APY](/methodology/calculating-apy)) |
| `tvl.usd` | TVL in USD |
| `score.vaultScore` | Reputation Score (0 to 100; observed 27.5–99.1): protocol TVL history 45 %, vault TVL history 25 %, holder distribution 15 %, asset 15 %, minus penalties ([Reputation Score](/methodology/reputation-score)) |
| `warnings[]`, `flags[]` | Risk signals; many integrators hide vaults with warnings (`allowVaultsWithWarnings=false`) |
| `isTransactional` | Whether the Transactions API can build deposits for the vault |

## Benchmark context

Each network has a USD and an ETH benchmark: the TVL-weighted APY of the five largest vaults of that denomination with at least $1M TVL ([benchmark rates](/methodology/benchmark-rates)). Integrators and analysts compare vaults against it; the [vault history vs benchmark](/recipes/vault-history-vs-benchmark) recipe shows the call.

## Holders and flows

The portfolio endpoints are per wallet: they show one user's positions, not your vault's depositor base. For vault-level data:

- `holdersData.totalCount` and `holdersData.topHolders[]` on `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}`,
- `GET /alpha/{network}/{vaultId}/holders`: holder list with balances (Alpha),
- `GET /alpha/historical/{network}/{vaultId}/daily-flows`: daily inflows and outflows (Alpha).

Alpha endpoints may change without notice ([versioning](/api-reference/versioning)).

## Attribution of partner flows

Deposits built through the Transactions API, or tagged with the attribution suffix, are attributed to the integrating account. Attributed TVL is shown in [portal.vaults.fyi](https://portal.vaults.fyi) by protocol, curator, vault and user; it is not available through the API (large partners can ask the team about API access). See [TVL attribution](/concepts/tvl-attribution).

# Glossary
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/resources/glossary

> Every vaults.fyi term with a one-line definition, the exact response field or parameter, the endpoint that returns it, and its unit: APY windows, base units, vaultId, score.vaultScore, positions, transactions, x402.

This page defines the terms used across the vaults.fyi docs and API. For each term it gives a one-line definition, the exact field or parameter name, the endpoint that returns or accepts it, and the unit. Use it to map a word in a question ("reward APY", "position size", "queued withdrawal") to the field you must read. All REST endpoints live at `https://api.vaults.fyi` and need the `x-api-key` header. Units follow three rules everywhere: APY and rates are **decimal fractions** (`0.0316` = 3.16%), token amounts are **integer strings in base units** (1 USDC = `"1000000"`), and timestamps are **Unix seconds**. Full rules are in [API conventions](/api-reference/conventions); status codes are in [Errors](/api-reference/errors).

Field paths use dots for nesting and `[]` for arrays. `<window>` is one of `1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day`.

## Vaults and identifiers

| Term | Definition | Field(s) / parameter | Endpoint | Unit |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| Vault | A yield-bearing product users deposit into and withdraw from (ERC-4626 vault, lending market, staking token, RWA token). | `data[]` items | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}` | — |
| vaultId | The identifier used in every `{vaultId}` path parameter. Usually the contract address; can be a non-address id (for example `0x5597...378c-25`). Copy it, never construct it. | `vaultId` | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/vaults` | string |
| Vault address | The vault contract address. Not always equal to `vaultId`. | `address` | same as above | `0x` address |
| Network | The chain a vault is on. Send the slug or the CAIP-2 id; a bare chain id or `ethereum` is rejected (use `mainnet`). | `network.name`, `network.networkCaip`, `network.chainId`; params `network`, `allowedNetworks` | `GET /v2/networks` | slug (`base`) or CAIP-2 (`eip155:8453`) |
| Default network scope | The four networks searched when `allowedNetworks` is omitted: `base`, `mainnet`, `arbitrum`, `optimism`. Applies to detailed-vaults and portfolio list endpoints, not to `GET /v2/vaults`. | `allowedNetworks` (repeat the key per value) | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/portfolio/positions/{userAddress}` and other portfolio lists | — |
| Asset (deposit asset, underlying) | The token users deposit and withdraw. TVL and balances are denominated in it. | `asset.address`, `asset.symbol`, `asset.decimals`, `asset.assetCaip` | detailed-vault, portfolio and transaction responses | `assetCaip` is CAIP-19 |
| Asset group | Denomination class of an asset; picks the benchmark (`USD` → `code=usd`, `ETH` → `code=eth`). | `asset.assetGroup` | detailed-vault responses | `USD`, `ETH`, `BTC`, `EURO`, `OTHER` |
| LP token (share token) | The token a depositor receives for a vault position. | `lpToken.address`, `lpToken.symbol`, `lpToken.decimals` | detailed-vault and portfolio responses | `lpToken.decimals` can differ from `asset.decimals` (18 vs 6) |
| Protocol | The DeFi protocol behind a vault. `name` matches every product; `protocolId` matches one product and may contain spaces (URL-encode). | `protocol.name`, `protocol.protocolId`, `protocol.displayName`; params `allowedProtocols`, `disallowedProtocols` | `GET /v2/protocols` | string |
| Curator | The risk manager that allocates a curated vault's funds. | `curator.name`, `curator.displayName`; param `curators` | `GET /v2/curators` | string |
| Tag | Category of yield mechanism (for example `Liquid Staking`, `RWA`). | `tags[]`; param `tags` | `GET /v2/tags` | Title-Case string |
| Nested vault | A vault whose deposit asset is another tracked vault's share token (for example an Aave market for weETH). | `apyComposite`, `childrenVaults[]` | detailed-vault responses | — |
| Transactional vault | A vault for which the Transactions API can build calls. | `isTransactional`; param `onlyTransactional=true` | detailed-vault responses, `GET /v2/vaults` | boolean |
| App-featured vault | A vault shown on app.vaults.fyi. `best-vault` and `best-deposit-options` default to featured vaults only. | `isAppFeatured`; param `onlyAppFeatured` | detailed-vault responses | boolean |
| Corrupted vault | A vault whose indexed data is known to be unreliable. Excluded unless `allowCorrupted=true`. | `isCorrupted`; param `allowCorrupted` | detailed-vault responses | boolean |

## Yield

| Term | Definition | Field(s) / parameter | Endpoint | Unit |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| APY | Realized annual yield from the vault's onchain share-price change over a trailing window, **net of fees**, not clamped (negative and >1 values occur). | `apy.<window>.total` | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}` | decimal fraction |
| APY window | The trailing period an APY covers. Detailed-vault objects have five; the per-vault `/apy` endpoint and benchmarks have four (no `1hour`). | `<window>` key in `apy`; params `apyInterval` (`1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day`; default `7day`) and `sortBy` (`apy1day`, `apy7day`, `apy30day`, `apy90day`) | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}/apy` returns `1day`–`90day` at the top level | `1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day` |
| Base APY | Yield from share-price growth (interest, strategy returns, staking). | `apy.<window>.base` | detailed-vault responses | decimal fraction |
| Reward APY | Yield from reward-token incentives, converted into the deposit asset. Already included in `total`. | `apy.<window>.reward`; per token `rewards[].apy.<window>` (a number, not an object) | detailed-vault responses | decimal fraction |
| Total APY | `base + reward` for a window. Use this for display. | `apy.<window>.total` | detailed-vault responses | decimal fraction |
| Single-window APY | Portfolio and historical endpoints return one window, chosen with `apyInterval`, without the window key. | `apy.base`, `apy.reward`, `apy.total` | `GET /v2/portfolio/positions/{userAddress}`, `GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}` | decimal fraction |
| Composite APY (`apyComposite`) | Present only on nested vaults. `intrinsicApy` is the underlying vault's own yield; `totalApy` is this vault's APY compounded with it. | `apyComposite.intrinsicApy.<window>.{base,reward,total}`, `apyComposite.totalApy.<window>.{base,reward,total}`, `apyComposite.vault.vaultId`, `apyComposite.asset` | detailed-vault responses | decimal fraction |
| Fees | Performance, management, deposit and withdrawal fees. Already reflected in APY; display only, never subtract. | `fees.performanceFee`, `fees.managementFee`, `fees.depositFee`, `fees.withdrawalFee` | detailed-vault responses | decimal fraction |
| Share price | Assets per whole share. Not a field of the detailed-vault object. | `sharePrice` | `GET /v2/nrt/vault/{network}/{vaultId}/sharePrice`, `GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}/sharePrice`, portfolio events | number |
| Benchmark | TVL-weighted average APY of the five largest qualifying vaults of one denomination on one network. `code` is required. | `apy.<window>.{base,reward,total}`, `timestamp`; param `code` = `usd` or `eth` | `GET /v2/benchmarks/{network}`, `GET /v2/historical-benchmarks/{network}` | decimal fraction; Unix seconds |

## Size, risk and freshness

| Term | Definition | Field(s) / parameter | Endpoint | Unit |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| TVL | Total value locked in a vault. | `tvl.usd`, `tvl.native`; params `minTvl` (default `100000`), `maxTvl` | detailed-vault responses, `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}/tvl` | `usd`: whole-USD integer string; `native`: asset base units |
| Base units | The smallest unit of a token (wei for 18-decimal tokens). Human amount = base units ÷ 10^`decimals`. | every `*Native` field, `tvl.native`, `positionValueInAsset`, transaction `amount` | all | integer string; parse with `BigInt` / `int` |
| Capacity | Deposit cap and room left under it. 2^256−1 means uncapped. | `maxCapacity`, `remainingCapacity` | detailed-vault responses | asset base units |
| Reputation Score | One number per vault: 0.45 × `protocolTvlScore` + 0.25 × `vaultTvlScore` + 0.15 × `holderScore` + 0.15 × `assetScore` − `totalScorePenalty`, floored at 0. Higher means a longer, larger track record. | `score.vaultScore`; param `minVaultScore` (check `score.vaultScore` client-side if you need a strict threshold) | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/detailed-vaults/{network}/{vaultId}` | unrounded number from 0 to 100; observed 27.5–99.1 |
| Score components | The four weighted inputs and the deprecated network score (0 weight). | `score.protocolTvlScore`, `score.vaultTvlScore`, `score.holderScore`, `score.assetScore`, `score.networkScore` | same | number |
| Score penalty | Points deducted for active or decaying flags; only the largest penalty applies. | `score.totalScorePenalty`, `score.penaltyComponents[]` | same | points |
| Flag | A vaults.fyi notice about an incident or risk, with severity and expiry. | `flags[].content`, `flags[].severity` (`info`, `warning`, `critical`), `flags[].endDate`, `flags[].resolution` | detailed-vault responses | `endDate` Unix seconds; `4946619450` = no expiry |
| Warnings | Plain-text copies of active non-`info` flags. `allowVaultsWithWarnings` defaults to `true` on detailed-vaults and `false` on best-vault / best-deposit-options. | `warnings[]`; param `allowVaultsWithWarnings` | detailed-vault responses | string |
| Holders | Holder count and balances of the vault's share token. | `holdersData.totalCount`, `holdersData.totalBalance`, `holdersData.topHolders[]` | detailed-vault responses | balances in LP base units |
| Last update | When the hourly indexer last updated the vault. | `lastUpdateTimestamp` | detailed-vault responses | Unix seconds |
| NRT (near-real-time) | Chain state read on request instead of from the hourly index. | `totalSupply` (LP base units), `totalAssets` (asset base units), `sharePrice` (number), `underlyingAssetPrice` (number, USD) | `GET /v2/nrt/vault/{network}/{vaultId}` | as listed |
| Max drawdown (Beta) | Largest peak-to-trough drop in share price over 30, 90 or 365 days. | `maxDrawdown.mdd30days`, `maxDrawdown.mdd90days`, `maxDrawdown.mdd365days` | `POST /beta/advanced-analytics/{network}/{vaultId}` | decimal fraction |
| Composition (Beta) | Where a vault's assets are allocated. | `composition[].name`, `composition[].allocation`, `composition[].vaultId`, `composition[].asset.positionValueInAsset` | `POST /beta/advanced-analytics/{network}/{vaultId}` | allocation: decimal fraction; amounts: base units |

## API mechanics

| Term | Definition | Field(s) / parameter | Endpoint | Unit |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| API key | REST credential. Send it in `x-api-key`. The MCP server instead uses `Authorization: Bearer <key>`, which REST does not accept. | header `x-api-key` | all REST endpoints except `GET /v2/health` | — |
| x402 | Pay-per-request access without a key. A request with no key returns HTTP 402 with a payment challenge. | `x402Version`, `accepts[].amount`, `accepts[].scheme`, `accepts[].network`, `accepts[].asset` | any paid endpoint without a key | `accepts[].amount`: USDC base units (`"2000"` = $0.002) |
| Credits | Units each request costs against your plan. | — | see [Credits](/plans/credits) | credits per call |
| Rate-limit headers | Remaining quota on authenticated responses. | headers `x-ratelimit-limit`, `x-ratelimit-remaining`, `x-ratelimit-reset` | authenticated responses | requests; seconds |
| Pagination | List responses: read `nextPage`; it is **absent** (not `null`) on the last page; no total count. | `itemsOnPage`, `nextPage`, `data`; params `page` (from 0), `perPage` (default 50, max 5000) | `GET /v2/detailed-vaults`, `GET /v2/vaults`, `GET /v2/historical/{network}/{vaultId}` and other lists | — |
| Soft errors | Unrecognised filter values return HTTP 200 with fewer results and the value echoed here. | `errors.unsupportedNetworks`, `errors.unsupportedAssets`, `errors.unsupportedProtocols` | list and portfolio endpoints | — |
| Array parameter | Multi-value query parameter. Repeat the key (`allowedNetworks=base&allowedNetworks=arbitrum`); a comma list returns 0 results. | `allowedNetworks`, `allowedAssets`, `allowedProtocols`, `tags`, `curators`, `claimIds` | all list endpoints | — |

# FAQ
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/resources/faq

> Short answers to common vaults.fyi questions: 402 vs 401, why results come from only 4 networks, last-page detection, units of APY and amounts, data freshness, APY differences, and how vaults get listed.

This page answers the questions developers and agents most often ask about the vaults.fyi API and data: errors, missing results, units, freshness, how yields are calculated and how vaults are listed. Each answer stands alone and links to the page with full detail. REST requests go to `https://api.vaults.fyi` with the key in the `x-api-key` header.

## Using the API

### Why do I get HTTP 402 instead of 401?

A request **without** an `x-api-key` header returns **402 Payment Required** with an x402 payment challenge (`x402Version: 2`, price in `accepts[].amount`, USDC base units on Base). This lets agents pay per request without a key. Send `x-api-key: <key>` to use your plan instead. A request with an **invalid** key returns **401** (message "An API key is required"). `GET /v2/health` needs no key. `Authorization: Bearer` works only for the MCP server, not for REST. See [Errors](/api-reference/errors) and [x402](/ai-agents/x402).

### Why are results only from Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum and Optimism?

`GET /v2/detailed-vaults` and the `/v2/portfolio/*` list endpoints default to `allowedNetworks` = `base`, `mainnet`, `arbitrum`, `optimism`. Pass the networks you want, repeating the key: `allowedNetworks=base&allowedNetworks=plasma&allowedNetworks=bsc`. `GET /v2/vaults` and `GET /v2/assets` have no default scope. Network ids: [Supported protocols and networks](/concepts/supported-protocols).

### Why is a vault I know about missing from the list?

Common causes, in order: its network is outside the default four; its TVL is below the default `minTvl=100000` (USD; pass `minTvl=0`); it is marked `isCorrupted` (excluded unless `allowCorrupted=true`); you passed a comma-separated array (`allowedNetworks=base,arbitrum` returns 0 results; repeat the key instead); or a filter value was not recognised (check `errors.unsupportedNetworks`, `errors.unsupportedAssets`, `errors.unsupportedProtocols` in the 200 response). `allowedAssets` takes token symbols, not addresses.

### How do I know I reached the last page?

List responses are `{itemsOnPage, nextPage, data, errors?}`. On the last page `nextPage` is **absent** (the key is missing, not `null`). There is no total count. Loop while the key is present:

```python
page = 0
while True:
    body = requests.get(url, headers=H, params={**params, "page": page}).json()
    rows.extend(body["data"])
    if "nextPage" not in body:
        break
    page = body["nextPage"]
```

`/v2/portfolio/*` endpoints are not paginated. See [API conventions](/api-reference/conventions).

### Is APY a percentage?

No. APY is a decimal fraction: `0.0316` = 3.16%. It is in `apy.<window>.{base,reward,total}` with windows `1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day`, net of fees and not clamped (negative and very large values occur). See [Calculating APY](/methodology/calculating-apy).

### Are amounts in wei?

Token amounts are integer strings in the token's **base units** (the smallest unit, "wei" for 18-decimal tokens): 1 USDC (6 decimals) = `"1000000"`, 1 ETH = `"1000000000000000000"`. This applies to transaction `amount` parameters (a decimal such as `1.5` returns 400) and to response fields such as `tvl.native`, `balanceNative` and `positionValueInAsset`. USD values (`tvl.usd`, `balanceUsd`) are decimal strings. Timestamps are Unix seconds.

### Why does my positions total look far too high?

In position objects, `asset.balanceNative` and `asset.balanceUsd` are the wallet's idle balance of the deposit token, repeated on every position with that token. Use `asset.positionValueInAsset` (asset base units) or `lpToken.balanceUsd` (USD) for position size. See [Portfolio tracking](/concepts/portfolio-tracking).

### Does the API check balances or simulate transactions?

No. `GET /v2/transactions/{action}/...` returns a payload even for amounts far above the wallet's balance, and the `simulate` parameter is deprecated and ignored. Check `asset.balanceNative` from the context call and verify every action before signing. See [Transaction flows](/concepts/transaction-flows) and [Transaction safety](/ai-agents/transaction-safety).

### How do I filter by Reputation Score?

Pass `minVaultScore` to narrow the list. Check `score.vaultScore` client-side if you need a strict threshold. See [Reputation Score](/methodology/reputation-score).

### What are the rate limits?

Authenticated responses carry `x-ratelimit-limit`, `x-ratelimit-remaining` and `x-ratelimit-reset` (seconds until reset). Read them instead of assuming a number; limits depend on your plan. See [Plans](/plans/overview) and [Errors](/api-reference/errors).

## Data and methodology

### Why do yields on vaults.fyi differ from other sources?

vaults.fyi calculates every vault's APY the same way: realized yield from the change in onchain share price over trailing windows (`1hour`, `1day`, `7day`, `30day`, `90day`), net of fees. Protocol UIs often show forward-looking or instantaneous rates from their interest-rate model, which can differ, especially right after rates change. See [Calculating APY](/methodology/calculating-apy).

### How often does data update?

Vault data is indexed from chain once per hour. Each vault's `lastUpdateTimestamp` (Unix seconds) shows the last index. For current chain state between updates, use the near-real-time endpoints (`GET /v2/nrt/vault/{network}/{vaultId}` and its `/sharePrice`, `/totalAssets`, `/totalSupply`, `/underlyingAssetPrice` variants). See [Indexing onchain data](/methodology/indexing-onchain-data).

### How does vaults.fyi get its data?

It runs purpose-built contracts as static calls (no gas, no onchain writes) through Infura, Alchemy and Ankr RPC providers. One call can read a value and feed it into the next calculation at a single block height. Results are stored and used for APY, TVL and scores. See [Indexing onchain data](/methodology/indexing-onchain-data).

### What causes sudden APY spikes?

Some vaults post earnings in batches rather than continuously. When a batch lands after hours or days, the share price jumps and short-window APY spikes temporarily. Use `apy["7day"]` or `apy["30day"]` for these vaults.

## Listing and support

### How are new vaults added?

Based on customer requests, with priority for protocols with significant traction. Coverage started with stablecoin, ETH and liquid-staking vaults on established protocols (Aave, Compound, MakerDAO, Yearn, Lido stETH, Rocket Pool rETH) and has grown from there. Protocol teams can submit the [protocol request form](https://listings.vaults.fyi/); the team reviews each protocol before integrating.

### What does a vault need to be listed?

All key data must be readable onchain from the vault's contract address (or a set of addresses). The following are onchain values the vault must expose. They are **not** API field names.

- TVL, denominated in the underlying token
- Total supply of the vault's share (LP) token, if it has one
- Price per share (normally TVL ÷ total supply, or an equivalent growth indicator)
- Maximum amount any user can deposit
- Liquidity directly available for withdrawal
- Address of the underlying ERC-20 token
- Vault name
- Address of the share (LP) token
- Addresses of reward tokens, and their emission rate per second

ERC-4626 vaults expose most of this directly. If reading the data needs a chain of calls across contracts, contact the team with instructions.

### How do I request a vault or feature, or follow updates?

Contact [@vaultsfyi](https://twitter.com/vaultsfyi) on X or Farcaster, or submit the [protocol request form](https://listings.vaults.fyi/). Weekly API and app changes are in the [Changelog](/resources/changelog).

# Jumper Earn: Cross-Chain Yield Discovery Powered by vaults.fyi

> How Jumper Earn uses the vaults.fyi API to unify yield discovery, transaction execution, position tracking, and attribution across DeFi chains and protocols.

[Jumper Earn](https://jumper.exchange/earn/) is a personalized yield discovery and execution engine that moves users from idle assets to earning positions through a single, intent-based workflow. By integrating the vaults.fyi API, Jumper accesses standardized yield data, ready-to-sign transaction payloads, position tracking, and attribution infrastructure — without maintaining protocol-specific integrations.

![Jumper Screen4](https://mintcdn.com/vaultsfyi/hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8/images/jumper_screen4.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8\&q=85\&s=f6cda09a842337599b7dfa6425f2ff49)

## The Problem: Fragmented Cross-Chain Yields

Despite DeFi's growth, yield opportunities remain scattered across chains and protocols. Users face compounding sources of friction:

* **Discovery fragmentation:** Yields are spread across dozens of networks and protocols, each with different interfaces and standards.
* **Asset mismatch:** Users rarely hold the exact token a vault requires, making swaps or bridges a prerequisite.
* **Execution complexity:** Moving from an asset on Chain A to a vault on Chain B can require five or more manual steps: approve → swap → bridge → swap → deposit.
* **Data staleness:** Inaccurate or delayed yield data leads to failed or suboptimal transactions.

These frictions reduce the conversion from yield discovery to actual deposits and create substantial integration overhead for any application trying to unify the experience.

## How Jumper Uses the vaults.fyi API

### Market and Vault Data

Jumper uses vaults.fyi as its data layer for vault metadata and strategy descriptions, real-time and historical APY and TVL, and yield and incentive breakdowns. This standardized coverage enables consistent comparisons across protocols and removes the need for custom data pipelines per integration.

![Jumper Screen2](https://mintcdn.com/vaultsfyi/hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8/images/jumper_screen2.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8\&q=85\&s=43192fed93b85784a4d8411fd4ae575f)

### Transaction Payloads

For supported vaults, vaults.fyi provides ready-to-sign calldata for token approvals, deposits, and withdrawals — including multi-step flows where users enter a redemption queue. These payloads feed directly into Jumper's routing engine. For cross-chain or multi-step flows, Jumper orchestrates via LI.FI Composer, bundling actions into a single user-facing confirmation.

![Jumper Screen1](https://mintcdn.com/vaultsfyi/hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8/images/jumper_screen1.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8\&q=85\&s=ac77412e2151fb59365c614df02ea3c2)

### Position Tracking

vaults.fyi position endpoints power Jumper's portfolio visibility: detecting existing vault positions, tracking deposited balances, and supporting cross-chain views — without requiring direct integration with each protocol's indexing logic.

![Jumper Screen3](https://mintcdn.com/vaultsfyi/hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8/images/jumper_screen3.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=hj3ZsHX8FvK7H-F8\&q=85\&s=e1c59b80d13ff849fcee44ea2bc82893)

### Attribution and TVL Tracking

vaults.fyi provides infrastructure for measuring distribution and adoption. Jumper uses it to track inflows originating from Jumper Earn, monitor net TVL associated with its integration, and attribute deposits to specific vault providers. This creates a shared, onchain-based source of truth for both integrators and vault curators — eliminating the need for manually reported analytics.

## Example User Flow

1. **Wallet connection:** Jumper detects existing assets and vault positions.
2. **Yield discovery:** Relevant vaults are surfaced based on the user's holdings.
3. **Execution:** The user selects a vault and confirms. Swaps, bridges, and deposits are orchestrated using vaults.fyi payloads where applicable.

# Otus: Smart Yield Account Powered by vaults.fyi
Source: https://docs.vaults.fyi/resources/case-studies/otus

> How Otus uses the vaults.fyi API to power Smart Yield, an agent-managed stablecoin account backed by DeFi yield infrastructure.

[Otus](https://www.otus.io/) is building a consumer finance app around one account that can access DeFi yield. Users deposit stablecoins, and Otus manages allocation and rebalancing across an approved set of yield sources.

Otus uses vaults.fyi as the yield infrastructure behind that experience. The Otus team can focus on agent logic, embedded wallets, and consumer UX while vaults.fyi handles vault discovery, risk signals, transaction payloads, position tracking, and attribution across supported DeFi protocols.

## How it works

Smart Yield combines embedded wallets, agent decisioning, and vaults.fyi transaction infrastructure.

**Example User Flow**

1. A user opens a Smart Yield account and deposits stablecoins.
2. The Otus agent evaluates a whitelisted set of yield sources.
3. vaults.fyi provides vault metadata, market data, and Reputation Scores to help narrow the approved universe.
4. vaults.fyi returns ready-to-sign transaction payloads for deposits, rebalances, and withdrawals.
5. Otus signs and submits transactions through its Privy-powered wallet experience.
6. vaults.fyi portfolio endpoints track where funds sit and what each user has earned.

![Otus Case Study Screenshot 1](https://mintcdn.com/vaultsfyi/aTXppGt3_h2pc549/images/otus_case_study_screenshot.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=aTXppGt3_h2pc549\&q=85\&s=ab6aab2a028972277857645300c45cab)

The result is a single user-facing balance backed by multiple DeFi vaults, with the custody and signing experience kept separate from the yield infrastructure.

## API capabilities used

The Otus integration uses several vaults.fyi API categories:

* **Vault discovery:** Live and historical APY, TVL, protocol, asset, fee, and metadata across supported EVM vaults.
* **Reputation Scores:** Protocol, network, and vault-level signals used to narrow the agent's approved universe.
* **Transaction payloads:** Ready-to-sign deposit, redeem, and claim transactions that Otus routes through its wallet stack.
* **Portfolio tracking:** Unified position and return data across the underlying protocols a user may be allocated to.
* **TVL attribution:** Reporting on deposits routed through Otus, with vault-level and address-level detail for reconciliation.
* **Referral and rebate support:** Attribution data that can support referral and rebate programs where available.

## Risk scoring and curation

Otus does not expose the full DeFi yield universe directly to users. It filters vaults into a conservative set focused on established lending and savings protocols, including over-collateralized money markets. Reputation Scores help the Otus agent evaluate protocol health, network health, and vault history before a strategy can enter the approved set.

Otus focuses on saver-style yields from established markets and treats unusually high-risk sources as out of scope.

## Why Otus built this with vaults.fyi

Smart Yield depends on breadth and reliability. The agent needs current data, risk context, transaction execution, and position tracking across many protocols, not a single hardcoded vault. Building that in-house would mean maintaining protocol-specific integrations, monitoring rate changes, normalizing portfolio data, and building attribution workflows across multiple DeFi systems.

With vaults.fyi, Otus gets one API layer for discovery, risk filtering, transactions, portfolio tracking, attribution, and referral or rebate support. That lets the team keep its engineering focus on the agent and account experience.
