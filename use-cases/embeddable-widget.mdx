## Match your product’s brand

![Widget with config](/images/widget_full5.png)

Configure the widget’s presentation to fit your application:

- Light or dark mode
- Accent color
- Font and corner radius
- Widget title
- Optional vaults.fyi attribution
- Visible columns for APY, TVL, protocol, curator, score, redemption type, balances, and returns

## Support discovery, positions, and transactions

The widget can present a compact Discover experience, a user’s existing positions, or both.

For transaction-enabled integrations, it supports host-controlled signing and custody handoff flows. Your product keeps control of the wallet experience while vaults.fyi provides the underlying opportunity data and transaction context.

## Built on vaults.fyi coverage

The widget uses vaults.fyi’s normalized vault data and transaction infrastructure across supported EVM networks and Solana. Teams can keep their interface focused while extending coverage as their product grows.
